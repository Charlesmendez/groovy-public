# Commercial Use

Any company, employer, client, team, organization, internal business, commercial, hosted, managed-service, revenue-generating, or production use of Groovy requires a Groovy Enterprise license.

Groovy Personal is not valid for:

- Work performed for an employer.
- Client projects.
- Company operations.
- Team use.
- Internal business tools.
- Revenue-generating projects.
- Customer-facing services.
- Hosting for third parties.

Public source visibility does not grant commercial rights.

Contact sales for enterprise licensing.
