# Groovy Enterprise License Summary

Groovy Enterprise is for companies and organizations that need commercial rights, self-hosting, source access, internal modification, and support.

Enterprise is not self-serve through Stripe at launch. Customers should contact sales.

Enterprise customers may receive rights to:

- Run Groovy inside their own cloud, VPC, data center, or approved environment.
- Use Groovy for internal business operations.
- Modify and maintain Groovy internally.
- Give employees, contractors, consultants, and service providers access as needed to operate Groovy.
- Receive full source access if included in the agreement.
- Keep fallback rights to the last paid version if they do not renew.

Enterprise customers may not, unless explicitly authorized:

- Resell Groovy.
- Sublicense Groovy.
- Host Groovy for third parties.
- Offer Groovy as a competing service.
- Remove license controls.
- Enable token-consumption billing for customer resale.

Non-renewal:

- The last paid version may continue within final licensed limits when fallback rights apply.
- New versions, source snapshots, security patches, downloads, support, private registry access, and expansion rights stop.
