# Contributing

Groovy accepts contributions through the public delayed source-available GitHub mirror and, for enterprise customers, through private patch or fork workflows when included in the agreement.

Before contributing:

- Read `LICENSE.md`.
- Do not assume public source visibility grants usage rights.
- Do not include secrets, customer data, prompts, outputs, private documents, credentials, or proprietary data in issues or pull requests.
- Keep changes focused and include tests for behavior changes.

Contribution paths:

- Public contributors may open issues and pull requests against the public mirror.
- Personal paid users may develop from current paid source snapshots and submit patches or PRs when appropriate.
- Enterprise customers may submit private patches, private pull requests, or portal-uploaded patch bundles according to their contract.

Groovy may require a CLA or DCO before accepting contributions.
